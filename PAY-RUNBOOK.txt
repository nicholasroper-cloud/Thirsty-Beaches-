PAY-RUNBOOK.txt : what the owner does when card payment misbehaves
==================================================================
Card payment is OFF unless kiosk-config.env says PAY_ENABLED=true. Every step below keeps customers able to pay at the window.

1. Stop taking cards right now
   Create an empty file named  .tb\pay-off.flag  in the kiosk folder. The next card attempt is refused and customers are sent to the window.
   Delete the file to resume. (The Control repo's payEnabled:false does the same remotely: it must be the boolean false, not the text "false".)

2. A ticket shows CHECK READER, do not charge (or the kiosk said "We're checking your payment")
   The reader could not say whether the card was charged. The order is locked and no other card can be taken until it is settled.
   a) Wait about a minute with the reader on: it normally answers by itself and the tile turns PAID or COLLECT.
   b) If it stays on CHECK: open Clover Dashboard > Transactions and look for a payment for that amount at that time.
      If the kiosk's CRITICAL log line says the reader charged a DIFFERENT amount than the sale: the kiosk keeps that order on CHECK and will not decide
      for you. Collect the balance or void the payment in the Dashboard, then record the outcome with "charged" (kept as paid) or "not-charged" (voided).
   c) Stop the kiosk (Stop-Kiosk.bat). The tool refuses to run while the kiosk is running ("stop the kiosk first", exit code 3). If the kiosk was
      killed and the tool says the lock is stale, run it again with --force. In the kiosk folder run:
        node tools\pay-resolve.js --list
        node tools\pay-resolve.js <externalId> charged         (the customer was charged: the order becomes PAID)
        node tools\pay-resolve.js <externalId> not-charged     (not charged, or you voided it: the order goes to COLLECT)
      then start the kiosk again. The externalId looks like TBKORD000000001-1.

3. A ticket shows DOUBLE PAID: void one
   The customer was charged twice for one order. In Clover Dashboard > Transactions void ONE of the two payments, then:
        node tools\pay-resolve.js <externalId> ack-double
   (the status page keeps an alert until you do).

4. The kiosk says payment is held, or the status page shows "PAYMENT HELD" (catalog)
   Clover prices moved by more than half, or a mapped item is priced under 25 cents. Card payment is held and the old prices are still used.
   If the change is correct, create an empty file  .tb\catalog-accept.flag  : the next sync (within 15 minutes) takes the new prices and deletes the flag.

5. New county or tax rate
   Change the Clover default tax rate and CATALOG_TAX_RATES in kiosk-config.env together (restart the kiosk). Until they match, card payment stays off.

6. Lost or changed card reader
   Revoke the old pairing in Clover, delete flex-pairing.json, restart the kiosk and enter the new pairing code on the reader. Do not paste the pairing file anywhere.

The journal (.tb\pay-journal.jsonl) holds order ids, amounts, last 4 digits and states only. Never put the .tb folder in Drive or OneDrive.
